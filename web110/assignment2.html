Web Browsers Learning Summary

Install Multiple Web Browsers to Compare Different Programs

I compared Opera and Google Chrome, the two browsers I use most often. I mainly use Opera on my computer because I prefer its interface. Its sidebar gives me quick access to social media, bookmarks, and browsing history. In contrast, I use Google Chrome on my phone for quick searches, checking information, or finding podcasts. From this activity, I learned that choosing a browser depends on its features, design, and the device it runs on. I also learned that the browsing experience may be different on a computer and a mobile device. In the future, I could use this knowledge to see how the same website looks in different browsers and on different devices. This would help me notice if a page looks different or does not work correctly.

Configure Browser Extensions and Add-ons

Before completing this activity, I did not know that browser extensions could be disabled or completely removed. I learned how to find my installed extensions in Opera’s settings and how to turn them off when I do not need them. I also learned that disabling an extension is temporary, while removing it deletes it from the browser. This knowledge could help me keep Opera cleaner and more organized. It could also be useful if a website loads slowly or does not work correctly because I can disable an extension to check if it is causing the problem. In the future, I can regularly review my extensions and remove the ones I no longer use.

LearnFree Internet Tips: How Do I Check My Internet Speed?

I found this topic very useful, especially the last part, which explained what to do when an Internet connection becomes slow. I learned that I can check my Internet speed using platforms such as Speedtest or Google’s speed test. These tools show the download speed, upload speed, and response time. This can be especially useful when I want to download or upload something. I would not start a large download when my Internet is slow because it could take a very long time. The same applies when I upload something online because the process could take longer or be interrupted. I also learned some technical information: around 10 Mbps may be enough for basic activities, although the necessary speed depends on what I am doing. I could use this knowledge in my personal or professional life to organize my work better, save time, and avoid wasting my devices’ batteries.
