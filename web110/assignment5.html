Social Media Learning Summary

Review LearnFree: LinkedIn Basics

I found this activity interesting because it explains what LinkedIn is and how to use it effectively. LinkedIn is a professional networking platform where people can create profiles, present their experience and skills, connect with professionals and companies, and search for jobs. I already knew about this platform because it has been popular in Europe for years, and several friends found jobs through it. However, the tutorial explained how to create a professional profile and use features such as connections, skills, endorsements, and recommendations. I also learned that LinkedIn has its own language: people make connections, send invitations, follow companies, endorse skills, and ask for recommendations. Understanding these terms makes the platform easier to use. This information can help me create a polished profile without making common mistakes. In the future, I can present my web design skills and projects professionally and confidently and show companies that I am serious about my career.

TutorialsPoint: Social Networking – Instagram

For this activity, I chose Instagram because it is the social media platform I use most often. I normally use it for entertainment and different types of information, but I analyzed it from other perspectives. For online marketing, Instagram is a perfect candidate because businesses, artists, and individuals use it to advertise their products. Businesses with physical locations and independent sellers can publish photos and videos commercially and include links to their products or online stores. For online jobs, I have seen pages publish information about job openings. Following the right pages could provide opportunities, even though Instagram does not specialize in employment. For online news, Instagram is useful because its algorithm shows content related to topics users frequently view. However, users must be careful about fake news and verify the source. For chatting, I do not think Instagram is the best platform for long conversations or calls, but it is great for sending Reels to friends and laughing together. For sharing pictures, audio, and videos, I think Instagram is one of the best platforms because it provides a good interface for photos, Reels, music, videos, and personal stories. This activity made me reflect on how Instagram can be used not only for entertainment but also professionally, especially to advertise my own products and work.

Review Smart Insights: The Honeycomb Model

For this activity, I evaluated popular social media platforms using the seven elements of the Honeycomb Model: identity, conversations, sharing, presence, relationships, reputation, and groups. Before this activity, I had never considered comparing social media in this way. Based on my evaluation, Facebook and Twitter/X support all seven elements strongly, while other platforms have weaker areas. I was less familiar with Reddit because it is not as common in Europe, but I learned that it focuses on conversations and communities called subreddits. This activity helped me understand that each platform serves different purposes. In the future, I can use this model to choose the best platform for sharing my projects and communicating with potential clients.
