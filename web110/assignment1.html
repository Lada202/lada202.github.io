Internet Overview

Our lives are increasingly connected to the Internet, and it is difficult to imagine modern life without it. However, few people think about its technical side. From this activity, I learned that our devices communicate through interconnected networks around the world using TCP/IP protocols. IP addresses identify devices and connections, while the Domain Name System translates domain names that are easy for people to remember into numerical IP addresses. The Internet provides enormous advantages for communication and access to information. The modern world also depends heavily on online commerce and services, from individual purchases to the enormous number of orders processed every day. Despite these advantages, this activity reminded me of online risks, including the loss of personal data and unreliable information. I will use the Internet in both my personal life and future career. For example, I use Pexels to find images for Photoshop projects and online research to find information and creative inspiration. This knowledge will also help me evaluate online sources more carefully and protect my personal information when using web resources.

How to Organize an Electronic Filing System

I found this activity very useful and practical because well-organized work is usually easier to manage and complete successfully. From this activity, I learned how to create a main folder and divide it into subfolders for different types of files. I also understood the importance of using clear and meaningful names so that documents are easier to find. I will apply this knowledge directly to my area of study because one of the first things we learned was that images and design files should be carefully organized and placed in the appropriate folders. For example, I can separate original images, Photoshop files, drafts, and final projects. I also learned the importance of creating backups in the cloud or on an external drive to protect my documents if my computer or phone has a problem. These methods will help me work more efficiently, avoid losing important files, and save time.
